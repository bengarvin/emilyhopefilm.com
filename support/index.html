Support

Help us finish Emily Hope

Emily let us film the last years of her life because she believed it matters how we talk about death. Your support helps bring her story to the people who need it.

Where your gift goes

The film is in the final stages of post-production. Contributions help cover the costs of finishing and sharing it, including hand-drawn animation by Squawk Productions, an original score by Andy Thompson, final sound and color, and festival and community screenings.

Give through FilmNorth

Emily Hope is a fiscally sponsored project of FilmNorth, a Minnesota nonprofit that supports independent filmmakers. Gifts made through FilmNorth are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
