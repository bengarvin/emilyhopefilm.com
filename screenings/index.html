Host a screening

We hope Emily Hope opens conversations about death, grief and honesty with children. We welcome screenings with hospice and palliative care teams, death doulas and end-of-life educators, green burial and home funeral groups, adoption and family organizations, faith communities, schools and community groups.

To bring the film to your organization or community, write to us at bengarvin@gmail.com or lseavert@gmail.com and tell us a little about your audience.

With End in Mind and its founder, Cathy Wurzer, we are planning a community screening tour across Minnesota, with conversations about grief, memory, legacy and love. Tour dates will be posted here.

Content note. The film gently includes moments from the final stage of Emily Hope Hofher’s life, shared in keeping with her wish to invite others into the reality of dying and to open conversation about something that touches us all. Please care for yourself in whatever way feels right.
