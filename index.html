Emily Hope

A Tiny Window Pictures film

An intimate vérité documentary about a mother racing against time to prepare her young adopted daughter for a future without her.

Directed by Ben Garvin and Lindsey Seavert. Now in post-production; festival premiere to be announced.

“Because everybody’s gonna die. And how you die and how you’re celebrated in the end, I think it really matters.”

Emily Hope Hofher

The story

Emily Hope began as a film about green burial and the ways we prepare for death. But Emily, a Minneapolis mother living with terminal illness, was facing a harder question: how do you prepare your young daughter for a life without you?

Filmed intimately over several years, the film follows Emily, her daughter Ruby and her husband Rob as they navigate what it means to prepare for loss while continuing to live. It became less a film about dying than about parenting, love, honesty, and the courage it takes to tell the truth.
